Privacy Policy

Apex Calc

Effective date: 6 November 2026.

This policy explains what Apex Calc does with information. The same text is shown in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy.

Public page: https://mobile-policies-hub.github.io/codex-apps/apex-calc/privacy.html

Who we are

Apex Calc is a utility you install from the App Store. Support is dev.outbox.88@outlook.com. The app does not ask you to create an account.

Information stored on this device

Apex Calc stores the numbers you enter, saved notes, and your language choice on this device. That information is not sent to us.

Calculator figures

Currency figures in the calculator are sample rates for reference only. They are not a live market quote and are not fetched from a market service.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier, or a usage profile. It does not use third-party analytics or advertising. On launch it does not request a remote configuration and does not open a remote page.

The app does not sell personal information and does not share it with data brokers.

Children

Apex Calc is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long information stays

On-device records stay until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local storage from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be shown in Settings and on this page. The effective date will change with it.

Contact

Questions: dev.outbox.88@outlook.com
