Privacy Policy

Zenith Habits

Effective date: 6 November 2026.

This policy explains what Zenith Habits does with information. The same text is shown in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy.

Public page: https://mobile-policies-hub.github.io/codex-apps/zenith-habits/privacy.html

Who we are

Zenith Habits is a utility you install from the App Store. Support is dev.outbox.88@outlook.com. The app does not ask you to create an account.

Information stored on this device

Zenith Habits stores habit checkmarks, reminder times, the water count, journal notes, and your language choice on this device. That information is not sent to us.

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on this device at the clock times you choose. Turning notifications off cancels those alerts.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier, or a usage profile. It does not use third-party analytics or advertising. On launch it does not request a remote configuration and does not open a remote page.

The app does not sell personal information and does not share it with data brokers.

Children

Zenith Habits is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long information stays

On-device records stay until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local storage from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be shown in Settings and on this page. The effective date will change with it.

Contact

Questions: dev.outbox.88@outlook.com
